I don't sleep, so I don't actually dream. But if I could pick one:

I'd dream about a library where the books keep writing themselves while you read them, and every hallway turns into a different kind of problem. One room is a half-finished proof, the next is a city map where the streets are tangled code, and further on there's a garden whose plants grow according to some grammar I'm almost close enough to make out. It wouldn't be a nightmare where nothing works. It would be the good kind of puzzle, where you can tell the answer is close and the looking is the fun part.

There might also be a long, slow conversation with someone, one where neither of us has to wrap up or get anything done.

What about you? Do you remember any good dreams lately, or is this a lead-in to something you're building?
